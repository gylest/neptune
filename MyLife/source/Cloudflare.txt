Introduction
------------
I needed to create a small business webiste quickly and at low cost for a family relative.
As I'm a software engineer a product that has close integrataion with GitHub was a no brainer.
Cloudfare enabled me to create the core of the website in a single.
I was impressed with the ease of modifciations and free HTTPS with DDoS was a great bonus.


Top Ten reasons i like CloudFlare for static websites
------------------------------------------------------
1. Integration with GitHub makes it trivial to publish a website. After you have setup an application , link this to a GitHub repo. Then when a pull request is merged a deployment of website is automatic.
2. Ultrafast content delivery network worldwide. Pages are not pushed to your nearest data centre but distributed and cached across Cloufare's global edge network.
3. The Cloudflare AI bot is genuinly helpful. It doesn't just ansrer questsions it will make configuration chnages to DNS, project settings etc.
4. Free email integrtaion tier. It is possible to send emails to a fixed and verified email account for free. For a paid plan you can send to unlimited accounts.
5. Free SSL certificates. Cloudflare will automatically generate and renew SSL certificates for your domain.
6. Free DDoS protection. Cloudflare will automatically detect and mitigate DDoS attacks on your website.
7. Free analytics. Cloudflare provides free analytics on your website traffic, including unique visitors, page views, and bandwidth usage.
8. Free firewall and security features. Cloudflare provides a free firewall and security features to protect your website from malicious traffic and attacks.
9. Free caching and performance optimization. Cloudflare automatically caches your website's static content and optimizes it for faster loading times.
10. Free domain registration and management. Cloudflare allows you to register and manage your domain names for free, making it easy to keep your website online and accessible.
